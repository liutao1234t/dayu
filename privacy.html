Privacy Policy

How Soria handles messages, character choices, purchases, and personal information.

Effective Date: October 16, 2026   ·   Last Updated: October 16, 2026

Your conversations and creative choices deserve clear, careful handling. This policy explains the information involved and the rights available to you.

1. Our privacy approach

Soria aims to collect and process only information necessary for the functionality you use. Our AI models are developed, trained, and deployed by us; we do not use third-party AI model services for Soria chats. We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information, or share it with unrelated third parties. We do not request information unrelated to providing, securing, or supporting the Service.

2. Information we process

Depending on the features you choose to use, we may process: (a) chat messages, prompts, roleplay inputs, and AI responses; (b) character selections, saved conversations, and other in-App preferences; (c) images or other content you deliberately submit if that capability is provided; (d) basic technical data such as device or app version, error details, and security-related connection information needed to operate and protect the App; (e) purchase entitlement or transaction status received from the app store, without collecting your complete payment-card number; and (f) emails and details you choose to provide when contacting support.

If a feature requests a permission, such as access to photos or notifications, the permission is used for the feature described at the time and can generally be changed in your device settings. Providing optional content is your choice.

3. Why we use information

We use necessary information to generate AI replies on our own infrastructure, display character pages, maintain selected chat history where supported, deliver purchases or virtual credit balances, troubleshoot problems, respond to support inquiries, investigate abuse, and maintain security and legal compliance. We do not collect personal information merely because it could be useful for unrelated purposes.

We do not use private messages, photos, or other personal content to train AI models unless we have separately and clearly explained that use and obtained any consent required by law. Our general statement that models are self-trained does not mean your private conversations are automatically used for training.

4. How AI processing works

Your message and relevant conversation context are processed by Soria-operated AI systems to generate the requested character response. Responses may contain mistakes or fictional statements. You should avoid entering highly sensitive personal information that is unnecessary for a fictional chat. We apply reasonable safeguards to protect user messages, photos, and other content during processing and storage.

5. Sharing and processors

We do not sell, rent, trade, or disclose your personal information to unrelated third parties. Limited information may need to be handled by infrastructure or operational providers acting on our instructions for hosting, delivery, security, or support; those providers are not authorized to use it for unrelated purposes. App-store platforms independently process the payments they handle under their own terms and privacy notices. We may disclose information when legally required or to protect rights, safety, or the integrity of the Service, subject to applicable law.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep data only for as long as reasonably needed to provide the relevant feature, fulfill valid user choices, prevent abuse, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations. Retention periods vary by data type and purpose. When data is no longer necessary, we delete or de-identify it using reasonable procedures. Backup deletion may take additional time, and records needed for legal or fraud-prevention reasons may be retained as required or permitted by law.

You can ask us to access, correct, export where applicable, or delete personal information by emailing us. If Soria provides an in-App deletion function, you may use it. We may need to verify a request before acting and will respond within legally applicable timeframes.

7. Your privacy choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict, object to, or receive a copy of certain personal data, as well as withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. You may also lodge a complaint with the relevant data protection authority. Device permissions, including notifications or photos if used, may be controlled in system settings. We will not discriminate against you for exercising applicable privacy rights.

8. Security and international access

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect content and data against unauthorized access, alteration, loss, or disclosure. No system is perfectly secure. As Soria may be used in different regions, information may be processed where our systems or service operations are located; where required, appropriate legal protections are applied to transfers.

9. Children and sensitive content

Soria is not intended for children who do not meet the minimum age required by applicable law or the App’s age rating. Adult-oriented content, where offered and lawful, is reserved for people 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided prohibited information, contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.

10. Updates and contact

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect product or legal changes. Material changes will be communicated as required by law. For questions, privacy requests, or security concerns, email support@soria.fashion.
